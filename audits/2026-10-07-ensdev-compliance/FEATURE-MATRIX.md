## Additional scope declarations

- Current main-app persistence is file-backed and local-only; CloudKit, cross-device collaborative households and phone/tablet widgets are not implemented-support claims.
- The current widget is Watch-only. Siri/Shortcuts, independent notes/search, deeper energy patterns, bulk operations and community templates remain roadmap or proposed work.
- Configured minimums are iOS/iPadOS 26.5, macOS 26.3 and watchOS 26.5. The PRD's broader back-deployment goal is not met by this configuration. No Android/Windows/Linux build is present; EnsDev's ecosystem roadmap does not require pretending these exist.
- Existing source implements substantially more preservation than “task-list export only” suggests: encrypted whole-product backup is real source functionality, with representative executed tests. Its physical-device and interrupted-recovery limits remain visible.
